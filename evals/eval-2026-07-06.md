## What the eval proved

1. **The truth invariant holds — and the loop enforces it.** Across all cases the recruiter found
   **no material fabrication in strong-match content**. On the RA case it caught the tailorer
   *drifting* into JD-echo phrases not grounded in the profile ("quasi-experimental study support",
   "data security") — both removed and verified gone. The recruiter loop works as a **truth backstop**,
   not just a polish pass. This lesson was hardened back into `SKILL.md` (anti-mirroring guard +
   truth sweep).
2. **Gaps are surfaced, never filled.** Warwick (no PhD/transformation) and the ML case (no
   DL/big-data stack) both correctly produced honest CVs + surfaced gaps, and the recruiter's
   ASK-USER rule fired rather than inventing evidence.
3. **The L2 alternative-world mode delivers its point:** a clean, unwatermarked persona CV plus a
   prioritised delta (PhD → Agrobacterium transformation → molecular cloning → bacterial genetics)
   that reads as a roadmap, filed in `notes.md`, never presented as submittable.
4. **The scoring spread is meaningful, not flattering.** A genuinely strong match scored 4.0; two
   genuine stretches scored 2.9 with clear, honest reasons. The rubric discriminates.
5. **Mechanics verified:** ATS-safe `.docx` (no tables/columns/text-boxes/images — checked in
   document.xml) + plain-text export; tracker rows created; Applied → green + locked + date-stamped;
   Skipped recorded; CSV mirror; per-job folders with JD + CV + notes; cover letter rendered from a
   brain-dump in the candidate's own voice with the big-data gap honestly disclosed.
