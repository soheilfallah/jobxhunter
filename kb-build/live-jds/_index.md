## Recurring structural observations (feed into jd-analysis.md)

- Application screening questions are common and act as hard knockouts: visa/right-to-work status, SIA licence, "can you start immediately", "have you attached a covering letter", years-of-experience minimums.
- UK postings frequently state salary as a range (£/year or £/hour); university roles state exact bands (e.g. Imperial £43,863–£47,223).
- "Right to work in the UK / no sponsorship" appears explicitly in university and public-sector posts.
- NHS/university JDs are long and duty-enumerated; startup/agency JDs are short and keyword-dense; agency posts (Huxley/SThree) sometimes duplicate whole paragraphs.
- Hybrid patterns are stated precisely (days per week in office) and matter for tailoring location lines.
