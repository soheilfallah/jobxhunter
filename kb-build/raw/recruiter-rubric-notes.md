## Eye-tracking / six-second scan
- Ladders eye-tracking study (2012: 6.0s; 2018 update: 7.4s average initial screen).
  - PDF: https://www.theladders.com/static/images/basicSite/pdfs/TheLadders-EyeTracking-StudyC2.pdf
  - Summary: https://www.theladders.com/career-advice/you-only-get-6-seconds-of-fame-make-it-count
  - HR Dive coverage: https://www.hrdive.com/news/eye-tracking-study-shows-recruiters-look-at-resumes-for-7-seconds/541582/
  - HR Daily Advisor: https://hrdailyadvisor.com/2018/11/15/eye-tracking-recruiters-average-7-4-seconds-reviewing-a-resume/
- Scan path: current title + company → previous title/company → dates (checking progression) → education. F/E-pattern reading; bold titles + bulleted accomplishments win; killers = clutter, no white space, multiple columns, long sentences.
- Kickresume survey: 62% of recruiters admit rejecting candidates without fully reading the CV, usually because a key requirement was missing early. https://www.kickresume.com/en/press/resume-trends-survey-cv-screening/

## ATS (UK)
- ~98–99% of large orgs use ATS/automated screen; large share of CVs never reach a human. Onrec: https://www.onrec.com/news/news-archive/will-your-cv-beat-the-bots-majority-of-uk-job-seekers-don%E2%80%99t-know-the-answer
- UK gov JobHelp guidance on ATS filters: https://jobhelp.campaign.gov.uk/improve-your-chances-of-getting-a-job/cv-job-applications-interviews/make-your-cv-stand-out/getting-through-application-filters-applicant-tracking-systems/
- Parse → structured data → keyword/qualification match vs JD → relevance score → threshold cut. Synonym failure is the classic loss ("stakeholder management" vs "worked with senior leaders").
- UK platforms: NHS Jobs/Trac, Civil Service Jobs, Reed, CV-Library, Hays; Workday/Greenhouse/Oleeo for corporates. A4, .docx/PDF, DD/MM/YYYY dates.
- ATS-safe: standard headings, single column, no tables/text boxes/headers-footers for key data, real text not images.
- Jobscan (match-rate approach vs JD): https://www.jobscan.co/
- iCover UK ATS guide: https://www.icover.org.uk/applicant-tracking-systems-uk/

## Rejection reasons / red flags
- Coburg Banks (UK agency) 20 rejection reasons: https://www.coburgbanks.co.uk/sales-recruitment-agencies/20-reasons-why-your-cv-will-be-rejected
- Indeed UK — 10 CV red flags (employer view): https://uk.indeed.com/hire/c/info/ten-resume-red-flags
- Recurrent themes: unexplained gaps, dates that don't add up, job-hopping/short tenures, typos/grammar, vague duties with no outcomes, no tailoring to the JD, missing contact/basic info, gimmicky formats/photos/odd fonts, inconsistent titles, over-claiming vs evidence.

## AI slop / authenticity
- Insight Global 2025 AI in Hiring survey: 88% of hiring managers believe they can tell when candidates used AI; 54% care.
  - Via: https://aiapply.co/blog/can-employers-tell-if-you-use-ai-for-a-cover-letter and https://www.rippling.com/en-IE/blog/ai-screening-crisis-hiring-cvs
- Giveaways: interchangeable polished-hollow language, "spearheaded/leveraged/dynamic", claims of "significant growth"/"enhanced efficiency" with no numbers, perfectly parallel vague bullets. Real people include verifiable specifics (named tools, datasets, orgs, figures).
- HR Grapevine / CV-Library: AI screeners may paradoxically favour AI-written text — so human-pass authenticity still the differentiator. https://www.cv-library.co.uk/recruitment-insight/academic-research-warns-ai-hiring-tools-may-favour-ai-written-cvs/

## NHS / public-sector shortlisting
- Person specification: essential vs desirable criteria; panels score each criterion from evidence in the application. Typical grids 0–2 or 1–3 per criterion (0 = no evidence, 1 = mentioned not evidenced, 2 = clear evidenced example).
- NHSBSA "How to score applications in NHS Jobs": https://www.nhsbsa.nhs.uk/sites/default/files/2023-01/How%20to%20score%20applications%20in%20NHS%20Jobs%20user%20guide.pdf
- NHS England shortlisting: https://medical.hee.nhs.uk/medical-training-recruitment/medical-specialty-training/overview-of-specialty-training/shortlisting
- HEE guide to effective applications: https://london.hee.nhs.uk/sites/default/files/application_forms.pdf
- Implication: for NHS-family roles the critic must map EVERY essential criterion to explicit evidence; missing one essential ≈ automatic screen-out regardless of overall quality.

## UK CV conventions
- 2 A4 pages norm; personal statement/profile (3–5 lines, what you offer, tailored); NO photo, DOB, marital status, nationality (Equality Act 2010); British English; DD/MM/YYYY or "Mar 2024 – Jun 2026" dates; references on request (omit).
- CVcorrect UK/US differences: https://www.cvcorrect.com/guide/cv-differences-usa-uk
- Resumemate UK CV format: https://www.resumemate.io/blog/uk-cv-format-2025-length-sections-examples/

## Academic / research-assistant screening
- PIs scan for: named techniques (PCR, ELISA, microscopy, R/Python stats), lab/PI context, publications/posters (even co-author), quantified throughput (samples, datasets), evidence of protocol discipline + reliability.
- resumelab UK RA CV: https://resumelab.com/uk/cv-examples/research-assistant
- Indeed RA guidance: https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/research-assistant-resume
