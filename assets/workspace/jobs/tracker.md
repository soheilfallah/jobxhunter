# Tracker

One row per advert ever seen. The search uses it to avoid showing you the same job twice, and it is
your record of where each application stands.

Verdict is the search's call: A, B, skip (with the reason) or unread.
Status moves: Kept or Skipped or Unread, then Tailored, Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected.
Found means an advert was collected but not judged yet; the next search judges it first.
