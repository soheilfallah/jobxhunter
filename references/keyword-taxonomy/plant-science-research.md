# Keyword taxonomy — Plant science / research technician (UK, entry–mid)

Use this as a KEYWORD PALETTE: match JD terms to REAL evidence in the candidate's profile. Never
invent a technique the candidate has not done. If the JD demands a skill absent from the profile,
the tailorer flags a gap — it does not fabricate it.

**Weighting** (higher = pull first when both JD and profile support it):
- `W3` TITLE-MATCH — appears in/defines the role title; strongest signal.
- `W2` MUST-HAVE — "essential"/"required"/degree/licence gate.
- `W1` NICE-TO-HAVE — "desirable"/"preferred"/"ideally".
- `[KO]` common KNOCKOUT — a binary gate; surface, never bluff.

Family scope: wet-lab plant biotech, plant breeding, plant pathology/crop protection, genomics,
and controlled-environment agriculture (CEA). Junior-to-mid: BSc/MSc technician and RA, NOT PI.

---

## HARD SKILLS
- `W2` Plant tissue culture / micropropagation — in vitro, aseptic technique
- `W2` Plant cell suspension culture — bioreactor / flask culture
- `W2` Molecular biology techniques (general) — cloning, extraction, analysis
- `W2` DNA / RNA extraction — nucleic acid isolation
- `W2` PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction) — standard, and qPCR (quantitative PCR / real-time PCR)
- `W2` Gel electrophoresis — agarose / SDS-PAGE
- `W2` Experimental design — controls, replication, randomised block trials
- `W2` Data recording & analysis — meticulous record-keeping, lab notebook discipline
- `W2` Metabolite extraction & analysis — analytical chemistry of plant compounds
- `W2` Glasshouse / greenhouse work — crop husbandry, growth-room management
- `W2` Field trial work — trial layout, marking out, treatment application, harvest/sampling
- `W2` Phenotyping — trait scoring, disease scoring, morphological measurement
- `W2` Microscopy & image analysis — light, fluorescence, confocal; imaging quantification
- `W1` Plant breeding — crossing, selection, germplasm/line maintenance
- `W1` MAS (Marker-Assisted Selection) — SSR (Simple Sequence Repeats), SNP (Single Nucleotide
  Polymorphism), RFLP (Restriction Fragment Length Polymorphism) markers, genotyping
- `W1` GBS (Genotyping-By-Sequencing) / library prep for sequencing
- `W1` CRISPR (Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats) gene editing
- `W1` Plant transformation — Agrobacterium-mediated, transgenics
- `W1` Plant pathology — disease assays, inoculation, pathogen/pest identification
- `W1` Crop protection — treatment/spray trials, agronomy
- `W1` Microbiology — culturing, plating, sterile technique
- `W1` High-throughput / machine-based phenotyping — drone / sensor / camera phenomics
- `W1` Hydroponics / CEA (Controlled Environment Agriculture) — nutrient dosing, environmental
  monitoring, vertical farming
- `W1` Bioinformatics (basic) — sequence analysis, handling sequencing datasets
- `W1` Statistical analysis of trial data — ANOVA, GenStat/R
- `W1` HPLC (High-Performance Liquid Chromatography) / GC-MS (Gas Chromatography–Mass
  Spectrometry) — metabolite/compound quantification
- `W1` Seed testing / germination / purity — ISTA-style protocols

## TOOLS / TECH
- `W1` R — statistical analysis of trial/phenotype data
- `W1` GenStat — agricultural statistics (common at NIAB/Rothamsted)
- `W1` Python — data handling / basic bioinformatics
- `W1` Microsoft Excel — data recording, analysis (table-stakes at technician level)
- `W1` Microsoft Office / SharePoint — Word, Excel; data management (NIAB-stated)
- `W1` ImageJ / Fiji — image analysis
- `W1` LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System)
- `W1` Fieldbook / trial-management software; GIS / drone imagery software
- `W1` qPCR instruments, thermocyclers, spectrophotometer (Nanodrop), plate readers
- `W1` Sequencing platforms (Illumina, Nanopore) — sample/library prep familiarity

## CERTIFICATIONS / LICENCES
- `W2 [KO]` Full UK driving licence — genuine gate for field roles (travel to trial/grower
  sites); NIAB states it explicitly. Own transport sometimes required.
- `W1 [KO]` Pesticide application certificates — PA1 (foundation) + PA6 (handheld) or PA9
  (near-ground); required for spray-trial roles.
- `W2 [KO]` Degree gate — BSc (or equivalent) in Plant Science, Biotechnology, Molecular Biology,
  Biochemistry, Agriculture, Horticulture, or related. Field match heavily weighted.
- `W1` MSc / PhD / postdoc — desirable, lifts to RA/scientist tier.
- `W1` BTEC / HND / A-levels in science or horticulture — accepted entry route for technician
  posts (NIAB).
- `W1` First aid; manual handling; COSHH awareness (lab/field safety)
- `W1 [KO]` UK right-to-work / work authorisation — stated in most institute posts.

## Notes for the tailorer
- This family rewards CONCRETE technique coverage + accurate record-keeping over prose. Map each
  JD duty to a specific technique the candidate has performed.
- Two sub-flavours: (a) **wet-lab / molecular** (tissue culture, PCR/qPCR, extraction, microscopy)
  vs (b) **field / breeding** (trials, phenotyping, spraying, driving + PA certs). Weight terms
  toward whichever sub-flavour the JD is in.
- Degree FIELD is a near-knockout — foreground a matching BSc/MSc early.
- Covering letter is frequently required (e.g. Hypocotyl) — check application questions.
- Candidate context: prior strawberry-breeding CV history → breeding/phenotyping/MAS terms are
  likely genuine profile matches; still verify against the actual profile before asserting.
