# Keyword taxonomy — Research assistant / associate (UK, entry–mid)

Use this as a KEYWORD PALETTE: match JD terms to profile evidence; flag missing must-haves as gaps.
Degree FIELD and TITLE match are the most heavily weighted signals in this family.

**Weighting:** `W3` TITLE-MATCH · `W2` MUST-HAVE (essential/degree gate) · `W1` NICE-TO-HAVE ·
`[KO]` common KNOCKOUT (binary gate — surface, never bluff).

Two sub-flavours, weight toward whichever the JD is in:
- **(a) Wet-lab / biomedical RA** — bench science, cell/molecular biology, manuscripts.
- **(b) Quantitative / evaluation RA** — research methods, coding, data, NHS/policy governance.

Scope: Research Assistant / Research Associate at university, NHS, institute, or foundation.
Junior-to-mid (Grade 5–7). "Lead" here means leading a workstream, not a faculty PI.

---

## HARD SKILLS
### Cross-cutting (both sub-flavours)
- `W2` Research methods — qualitative AND quantitative study design
- `W2` Data collection — structured, accurate, documented
- `W2` Data manipulation / cleaning / harmonisation — structure & characteristics of datasets
- `W2` Data analysis — statistical and/or thematic
- `W2` Literature review — reviewing technical/scientific documentation
- `W2` Accurate documentation & data security — record-keeping, reproducibility
- `W2` Academic writing — contributing to manuscripts, reports, summaries, proposals
- `W1` QA (Quality Assurance) of research / trial projects
- `W1` Project management — planning, risk identification, timelines
- `W1` Presenting findings — conferences, boards, commissioners, stakeholders

### (a) Wet-lab / biomedical
- `W2` Cell culture — mammalian / primary cell work
- `W2` Molecular biology — cloning, PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction), qPCR
- `W1` CRISPR (Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats) approaches
- `W1` Gene-expression analysis — RT-qPCR, RNA-seq handling
- `W1` Chromatin biology / chromatin-based assays — ChIP, ATAC
- `W1` Microscopy — confocal / fluorescence imaging
- `W1` Proteomics — sample prep, analysis
- `W1` Sequencing datasets & bioinformatics — NGS (Next-Generation Sequencing) data handling
- `W1` Protocol optimisation; general lab practices; cancer/cell biology basics

### (b) Quantitative / evaluation
- `W2` Programming for analysis — Python (preferred), R
- `W2` Statistical analysis — regression, inference, descriptive stats
- `W1` Mathematical / simulation modelling — infectious-disease, health-systems modelling
- `W1` Epidemiology — study design, inequalities analysis
- `W1` Service evaluation — outcome monitoring (activity, waiting times, engagement, recovery)
- `W1` Developing/maintaining analytical or simulation code (version-controlled)
- `W1` Survey design; interview/focus-group methods; NVivo-style coding

## TOOLS / TECH
- `W2` Python — analytical/simulation code (quant RA); `W1` R / RStudio
- `W1` SPSS / Stata / SAS — social & health science stats
- `W1` NVivo — qualitative coding
- `W1` Excel — data handling, table-stakes
- `W1` Git / version control — code maintenance
- `W1` LaTeX / EndNote / Zotero / Mendeley — writing & referencing
- `W1` REDCap / IAPTus / clinical data systems — NHS data capture
- `W1` PowerPoint — stakeholder presentation
- Wet-lab instruments: thermocyclers, confocal microscope, flow cytometer, Nanodrop, sequencers

## CERTIFICATIONS / LICENCES
- `W2 [KO]` Degree gate — first or master's degree in the RELEVANT field. Wet-lab: biological/
  biomedical science. Quant: epidemiology, mathematical modelling, statistics, computer science,
  public health, psychology, engineering. Field match is a near-knockout.
- `W1` PhD — desirable / required for Research Associate (vs Assistant) tier.
- `W1 [KO]` GCP (Good Clinical Practice) — required for clinical/NHS research delivery.
- `W1` Research governance / information governance / data protection (GDPR) awareness — NHS/uni.
- `W1` Minimum years' research experience (e.g. "2 years' practical research") — soft KO.
- `W2 [KO]` UK right-to-work / work authorisation — stated in most posts.

## GENUINE SOFT SKILLS
(Include only when JD-named and profile-evidenced.)
- `W2` Analytical & critical thinking
- `W2` Attention to detail — accurate data, protocol fidelity
- `W2` Written & verbal communication — manuscripts, reports, presentations
- `W1` Independence + collaboration (works solo and in a team)
- `W1` Organisation / time management across parallel projects
- `W1` Mentoring / training junior staff or students (lead-workstream tier)
- `W1` Confidentiality & integrity (clinical/participant data)

## TITLE VARIANTS
`W3` on title match:
- Research Assistant / Research Associate / RA
- Clinical Research Assistant / Clinical Research Practitioner
- Research Support Assistant / Research Officer
- Senior Research Associate / Research Associate (lead workstream)
- Postdoctoral Research Assistant (early PDRA) / Research Fellow (junior)
- Evaluation Officer / Research & Evaluation Officer
- Data / Modelling Research Assistant
- Laboratory Research Assistant / Scientific Research Assistant

## Notes for the tailorer
- Detect sub-flavour from the FIRST duty block and weight terms accordingly; do not mix wet-lab
  and quant vocabularies into one CV.
- University JDs quote exact pay bands (e.g. Imperial £43,863–47,223); these follow the New JNCHES
  pay spine (RA ≈ Grade 5/6, Associate ≈ Grade 7). London figures include weighting.
- NHS/university JDs are long and DUTY-ENUMERATED — map CV bullets one-to-one to listed duties;
  coverage breadth is scored.
- Foreground the matching degree field early; it is a near-knockout.
- GCP + information governance are genuine gates in clinical research — surface if held.
