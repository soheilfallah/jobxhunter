# Keyword taxonomy — Security (frontline, SIA-licensed) (UK, entry–mid)

Use this as a KEYWORD PALETTE: match JD terms to REAL profile evidence. NEVER claim an SIA licence
or clearance the candidate does not hold — the licence is a legal gate and a verifiable knockout.
Flag missing gates; never bluff them.

**Weighting:** `W3` TITLE-MATCH · `W2` MUST-HAVE · `W1` NICE-TO-HAVE · `[KO]` common KNOCKOUT.

Scope: PHYSICAL / frontline security (SIA-licensed) — officer, door supervisor, patrol, CCTV
operator, and assistant/site manager. This is DISTINCT from information/cyber security (infosec,
security vetting officer), which belongs to a different taxonomy. This family rewards CONCRETE
duty coverage + valid certs over prose.

## CERTIFICATIONS / LICENCES
- `W3 [KO]` SIA licence (Security Industry Authority) — the SINGLE hardest knockout; always front
  and centre. Specify sub-type required:
  - `SG` = Security Guarding
  - `DS` = Door Supervision (covers guarding of licensed premises)
  - `CP` = Close Protection (advanced; VIP/executive)
  - `PSS / CCTV` = Public Space Surveillance (needed for dedicated CCTV monitoring of people)
  DS/CP/SG cover incidental CCTV; a standalone PSS/CCTV licence is needed to monitor people/disorder.
- `W1` First Aid at Work (FAW) / Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW) — increasingly a licence-renewal
  prerequisite (2026 SIA rules).
- `W1` Mental Health First Aid (MHFA)
- `W1` FREC (First Response Emergency Care) — higher medical tier
- `W1` Counter-terrorism training — ACT Awareness, SCaN
- `W2 [KO]` Minimum experience — "minimum 1 year relevant security experience" common gate.
- `W2 [KO]` Physical fitness — stated requirement (patrols, standing, response).
- `W2 [KO]` UK right-to-work / no sponsorship — required; frontline pay at/above National Living
  Wage (£12.71/hr from 1 Apr 2026), typically £12.71–16/hr.
- `W1 [KO]` Full UK driving licence — required for mobile/patrol-driver roles.
- `W1` Environment experience — casino/gaming, events, retail (high-value), corporate, university,
  hospitality/licensed venues (named as desirable and often decisive).

## Notes for the tailorer
- Lead with the SIA licence and its EXACT sub-type — it is the first thing screened and a legal
  gate. If the candidate's licence type does not match, flag the knockout; never imply otherwise.
- After the licence, map CONCRETE duty coverage to the JD's duty list (patrols, access control,
  CCTV, incident reporting, fire/first-aid, emergency response). Breadth of duty match wins here.
- Environment experience (casino, events, university, retail) is often decisive — surface a match.
- Physical fitness, reliability, and clean record are genuine gates — state plainly if evidenced.
- Keep the register plain and duty-anchored; this family does not reward flowery prose.
- Pair acronyms: SIA (Security Industry Authority), SG/DS/CP/PSS (licence sub-types), CCTV
  (closed-circuit television), BS7984 (keyholding standard), SLA/KPI, COSHH, HSQE, ACT/SCaN,
  ANPR, EFAW/FAW.
