# Academic register — for research, PhD and fellowship targets

No `academic-prose` skill ships with this plugin, and none is assumed to be installed. This file
distils the register instead, drawing on journal house-style rules and the `humanizer` checklist.
If an academic-prose skill is installed on your surface, apply it as well; this file still holds.

Applies ON TOP of `writing-voice.md` and the humanizer pass, only when the target is academic:
a research assistant/associate post, a PhD application, a fellowship, a research-institute role.
It never applies to industry CVs.

## The register

- **Hedged claims, exact numbers.** "improved" needs a measurement; "significantly" needs a
  test. Write "reduced the error rate from 12% to 8%" not "dramatically improved accuracy".
  A claim without its number is a claim to delete.
- **Method-first sentences.** Lead with what was done and on what: "Ran <measurement> on
  <material> under <condition>", not "Passionate about cutting-edge <field>".
- **No promotional vocabulary.** The humanizer list applies doubly here: cutting-edge,
  state-of-the-art, novel (unless defending a specific novelty claim), pioneering, world-class
  — all out. Academic readers treat them as noise at best.
- **Named methods, named instruments, named statistics.** The instrument, the software, the
  test, the registration template. The names are the evidence.
- **Publications cited in full**, journal style: authors, title, journal, year, volume, pages.
  On an academic CV the publication list is a section, not a bullet.
- **References/referees ARE expected** — the one place they belong. Named referees with title,
  institution and email on the academic CV, and always in the application form's referee
  fields. (The profile's rules block can allow the references line on the research lane only —
  this is why.)
- **First person sparingly, passive tolerated.** Academic convention accepts "measurements
  were taken"; the ban on passive voice in `writing-voice.md` is relaxed here, not lifted —
  prefer active where it reads naturally.
- **Honest scope.** "Conceptual knowledge only" techniques (the profile's never-claim list)
  stay unclaimed exactly as on industry CVs. An academic reader will probe them at interview.

## Mechanics

- Length: an academic CV may exceed two pages once publications and referees are listed; the
  two-page rule in `uk-conventions.md` yields here.
- Grades on a non-UK scale are allowed where the profile's rules gate them to research lanes —
  give the scale: "<mark>/<max> (<institution>)".
- Research statements and cover letters for academic posts follow the same register: question,
  method, result, fit with the group's work. Read the group's two most recent papers before
  writing the fit paragraph; name one, honestly.
