Fills the gap to the job. Recommends the gaps to close.

jobxhunter searches live UK and Canada job boards, tailors an ATS-safe CV that fits each role from your real experience, then names the exact gaps worth closing. It drafts. You review and send.

The search does the digging for you.

It pulls live roles straight from Reed, Adzuna and Firecrawl, and falls back to web search when a board has no API. It reads the full job description behind Workday, Greenhouse and Lever links, not just the teaser. Point it at a workspace and the daily hunt sources every new role, triages the fits, tailors them and files them while you get on with your day. UK and Canada work out of the box.

ReedAdzunaFirecrawlIndeed / Diceweb-search fallback

It fits you to the role, honestly.

Every CV is built from one master profile of your real experience, and shaped for both the ATS parser and the six second recruiter scan. An independent recruiter agent scores each draft in its own context, seeing only the job and the finished CV, so the score is earned rather than self-graded. On one real UK role it came back 4.0 out of 5, ATS clean.

Then it recommends the gap to close.

Where your experience falls short of a role, jobxhunter does not paper over it. It shows you the missing piece, and in its alternative-world mode it sketches the ideal candidate for the job and the shortest path from you to them. A roadmap you can act on, never something it puts on your CV.

The whole hunt, in one view.

Every application lands in a locked tracker, and a local dashboard renders the funnel, the live status of each role and the spread of recruiter scores. Interview prep pulls the questions you are likely to face, with answers drawn from your real evidence.

Yours, on your machine.

Your profile and applications stay in a private folder that never leaves your computer. No API keys ship in the repo, you bring your own. It runs as a Claude Code plugin or through any AGENTS.md-compatible CLI, and it is source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License: free for your own use, not for resale.
